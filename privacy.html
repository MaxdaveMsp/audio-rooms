Privacy Policy

Audio Streaming Rooms (iOS listener app) · Last updated 10 November 2026

The Audio Streaming Rooms app lets you listen to live audio rooms streamed on your local Wi-Fi network, for example at a venue, conference or event. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: the app collects no personal data, has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, e-mail address, phone number or account of any kind.
  • No location data, contacts, photos or files.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking. Nothing is shared with third parties.

Local network

The app finds streaming computers on the Wi-Fi you are connected to (Bonjour) and receives their audio, room list, captions and messages over that local network. The app connects only to the streaming computer you choose. It does not send your audio anywhere — it only receives.

When you join a room, the app tells the streaming computer a listener name ("iPhone" or the name of your device) so the organiser can see how many people are listening. This stays on the organiser's computer and is not sent to us.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a room's QR code. The image is processed on your device to read the code and is never stored or sent.

Data stored on your device

The app remembers the streaming computers you used, room passwords you entered and your volume, on your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
