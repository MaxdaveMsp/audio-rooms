Audio Streaming Rooms (iOS listener app)

Audio Streaming Rooms lets you listen to live audio rooms — talks, translations, guided tours, silent events — on your iPhone or iPad over the local Wi-Fi.

How to listen

  1. Join the Wi-Fi network of the venue or event.
  2. Open Audio Streaming Rooms. The streaming computer appears by itself; tap it, then tap Listen next to your room.
  3. Or scan the room's QR code with the scanner button at the top of the app.

Nothing appears?

  • Make sure your iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi as the streaming computer (not a guest network or mobile data).
  • Allow Local Network for Audio Streaming Rooms in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  • Ask the organiser for the address and type it under "Type the address", or scan the room's QR code.

Sound stops or breaks up

  • Move closer to the Wi-Fi access point; walls and crowds weaken the signal.
  • Keep the app open or allow it to play in the background (it keeps playing with the screen locked).
